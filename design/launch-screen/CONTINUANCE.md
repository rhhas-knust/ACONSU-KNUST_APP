# Continuance

*An algorithmic philosophy for the ACONSU launch screen*

---

## The movement

**Continuance** holds that the most beautiful structures in a generative system are not the ones a single population leaves behind, but the ones that survive the population itself. A flow field can be rendered in a thousand ways; what almost no one renders is *succession* — the moment one generation stops moving and, in stopping, decides where the next will begin. Continuance makes that moment the subject. Everything else in the composition exists to give the handover somewhere to happen.

The algorithm runs in generations rather than frames. A cohort of light-bearing particles is born, drifts through a curl-shaped field of layered noise, and lays down luminous trails as it goes. Each particle carries a finite life. When that life expires the particle does not simply vanish and free a slot — it *seeds*. Its final resting position becomes the birthplace of one or two members of the cohort that follows, inheriting position and a fraction of heading, but never velocity: every generation must find its own momentum. The lineage is positional, not kinetic. What passes down is where you stand, not how fast you were going.

Between generations the field itself turns. A small rotation — a few degrees, tuned by hand over many iterations until it read as *drift* rather than *spin* — is applied to the underlying noise before the next cohort wakes. This is the quiet engine of the whole piece. Because each generation inherits its ancestors' positions but inhabits a slightly rotated world, the trails fan rather than repeat. Lineages bloom outward in soft arcs, and the accumulated image acquires depth that no single pass could produce. The rotation constant is the most carefully calibrated number in the system; too much and the generations shear apart into unrelated smears, too little and the whole composition collapses into one thick over-drawn line. The window between those failures is narrow, and finding it is the work.

Colour encodes time, never position. The earliest generations settle into deep, almost-black violet; each successive cohort is lifted a measured step toward lilac, so that reading the image from dark to light is reading it from oldest to newest. Gold is reserved — strictly, and this restraint is the piece's whole discipline — for the instants of handover themselves: the pinpoint where a dying particle seeds its successor. Nothing else in the composition is permitted to be gold. Because handovers cluster where the field goes quiet, the gold distributes itself into constellations the algorithm never places deliberately. They are consequences, not decisions. An implementation that scatters gold for decoration has misunderstood the movement entirely.

Density must be earned rather than dialled in. Trails are drawn at very low alpha and allowed to accumulate, so that brightness is a record of how many lineages chose the same path — an emergent consensus rather than an authored highlight. A master-level implementation resists every temptation to brighten the result directly; it adjusts population, lifespan and field coherence until the light arrives on its own. This is painstaking work, and it is the difference between a computational sketch and a composition that looks as though it cost someone months. Every threshold here — when a particle dies, how many successors it earns, how far a trail may travel before the field releases it — should feel inevitable in the final frame and be invisible as a choice.

The system is deterministic under seed, and this is not a convenience but a claim the movement makes: succession is not chaos. The same seed must yield the same lineages, the same constellations, the same bloom, down to the pixel — while a neighbouring seed yields something recognisably of the same family and entirely its own. That is the proof the algorithm is doing real work. Continuance is finished not when the canvas is full but when the last generation has laid its trails and the gold has found its places: an image of a thing handed on, unbroken, and never once handed on the same way twice.

## The seed beneath it

The union's own name carries the word. *Continuation* — a body that persists while every person in it is replaced, where each year's executive is vetted, serves one academic year, and hands over together. The algorithm is that constitution expressed as a system: positions inherited, momentum never; the field turned slightly for each new cohort so no one governs quite the world their predecessor did; and the brightest points in the whole composition reserved for the moment of handover rather than for anyone's tenure.

Those who know will see a handover. Everyone else sees light finding its way through a dark field.
