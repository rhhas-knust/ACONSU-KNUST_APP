Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This website belongs to , chapter ("we"). It is a public information page. We process personal data under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana. You can reach us about anything on this page at .

Short version

  • Reading this website does not give us any information about you. We use no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The only form on it is the alumni request form. It collects only what is needed to list you, and only when you press Send.
  • You can ask us to correct or remove what you sent at any time.

When you only read the website

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, a Microsoft company). Like every web host, GitHub receives your IP address and the details your browser sends when you load a page, and may keep them in its server logs. We do not receive or see those logs. GitHub explains what it does with them in its own privacy statement.

Fonts, images and scripts all come from this website itself. Nothing is loaded from Google or any advertising or analytics company. This website does not store anything on your device. See Cookies and storage.

Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form. Your name, photo and words are shown publicly only after an admin approves them.

Where it goes. When you press Send, your request goes to the ACONSU app, which runs on Render's servers with its database on MongoDB Atlas. These providers may store data outside Ghana. They act for us and may not use your data for their own purposes. An admin reviews your request there. If it is declined, your photo is deleted straight away.

What becomes public. Once you are approved, your name, photo, words, work and year appear in the app, and a scheduled job copies them to this website. That copy is kept in this website's public source code repository on GitHub. Your email or phone number is never copied.

Removing yourself. Email us and we will take you down from the app. The website follows at its next scheduled update, usually within three hours. Be aware that search engines, caches, and the repository's history can keep an older copy for longer than we control. We will remove what we can and tell you what we cannot.

How long. We keep your entry for as long as you want to be listed.

People named on this website

The chapter's coordinators have asked for their own phone numbers and email addresses to be published so that people can reach them. Executives and founders are shown by name, office and photograph. Anyone listed who wants a line changed or removed should write to us.

Links to other sites

Links to WhatsApp, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Telegram and Google Maps are ordinary links. Nothing from those services is loaded on this page, so they learn nothing about you until you choose to follow a link. After that, their own policies apply.

Your rights

Under Act 843 you may ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, stop using it, or withdraw consent you gave. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before you withdrew it. We will reply as soon as we reasonably can.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana (dataprotection.org.gh).

Children

The alumni form is for adults who have left the union. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian to agree before sending anything.

Security

This website is served over HTTPS. The alumni form sends your details only to the ACONSU app, over HTTPS. The app is protected by sign-in, access limits and rate limits. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affected you, we would tell you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date above changes. If a change affects how we use what you sent, we will ask you again.
