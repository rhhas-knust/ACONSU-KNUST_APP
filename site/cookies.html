Cookies and storage

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device: no local storage, no session storage, no fingerprinting, no analytics and no advertising identifiers. Because of that, there is no cookie banner and nothing for you to accept or refuse.

KindUsed here?
Strictly necessary cookiesNo
Preference cookies or local storageNo
AnalyticsNo
Advertising or social media trackersNo
Embedded videos, maps or like buttonsNo. Links only.
Fonts from other companiesNo. Fonts are served from this website.

What still happens

  • The host (GitHub Pages) sees your IP address when your browser asks for a page, as any web host does. See the privacy policy.
  • If you follow a link to WhatsApp, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok or any other site, that site may set its own cookies. Those are covered by its policy, not ours.
  • The ACONSU app is a separate service. It uses one sign-in cookie, and only after you sign in. Its own cookies and storage page explains it.

If this changes

If we ever add anything that stores data on your device and is not strictly necessary, we will ask for your permission first and update this page.
