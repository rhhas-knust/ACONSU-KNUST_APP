The steps in writing

  1. Find your way around The bar at the bottom has Home, Events, Bible, Prayer and More. Tap one to go there.
  2. Join Tap Log In at the top, then Sign up. Add a photo and type your name, email and a password. Say whether you are a student or an alumnus, tick the box to agree to the Terms of use and the Privacy policy, and tap Create Account. Your profile opens and you are signed in.
  3. Events Events shows what is coming up. If an event asks for a sign-up, tap Register Now and confirm your details.
  4. Bible Pick a book, a chapter and a version, and read. Reading on different days builds your streak.
  5. Prayer Write your request and choose who sees it. “Only leaders” keeps it private. The Prayer Wall shows it to the community, with your name or without it.
  6. More More holds the rest: departments, groups, community, giving, sermons and your account. Scroll to see all of it.
  7. Your profile In More, tap your name. Your details, your streak and your badges are there. The moon at the top switches between a light and a dark screen.
  8. Help The bell at the top shows announcements. If you are stuck, open More and then Contact Us, or ask your chapter leaders.
