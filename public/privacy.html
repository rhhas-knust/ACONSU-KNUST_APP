Your Information

Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and how to have it removed.

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy covers the ACONSU website and the ACONSU mobile app. Both are run by The Apostles' Continuation Students Union, KNUST chapter. The app is a view onto the same service as the website, so everything below applies to both equally.

The short version. We collect what we need to run the union's membership and pastoral care, and nothing more. We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone outside ACONSU except the service providers listed below who help us operate. Sensitive things — prayer requests, welfare cases and pastoral notes — are seen only by the specific office that handles them.

Who we are

ACONSU-KNUST is a student union chapter. The person responsible for data in this service is the Chapter Coordinator. You can reach us through the contact page, and the contact address shown there is the one that reaches whoever currently holds that office.

What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your name, email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash — we never hold the password itself)
  • Your phone number, if you give one
  • A profile photo, which is required at registration so members can recognise one another
  • Your chapter, and your programme, level and hostel or residence, if you give them
  • Your birthday — month and day only, never the year, so your age cannot be worked out from it

As you take part

  • The department you belong to, and your membership stage as it changes
  • Attendance recorded when your membership QR code is scanned at a service or department meeting
  • Events you register for
  • Sermon notes and Bible-reading progress you record, and your app streak
  • Messages you post in community chat or a group
  • Prayer requests and testimonies you submit
  • Welfare requests you submit

Kept about you by the offices that care for members

  • Shepherding keeps pastoral notes and a record of contact, so that whoever is walking with you knows your situation
  • Welfare keeps case notes on a request while it is being handled
  • Finance keeps records of giving and of money the chapter receives or spends

Technical

  • A session cookie, so you stay signed in. It is strictly necessary — the service cannot work without it — and it is not used for tracking.
  • If you turn on notifications, a push subscription identifying your browser or device to the push service. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Ordinary server logs, including your IP address, kept for security and to diagnose faults.
A note on what this reveals. ACONSU is a Christian students' union, so holding an account here indicates a religious affiliation. Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and equivalent laws elsewhere, that is a special category of personal data. We treat it accordingly: membership information stays inside your own chapter and is never shared outside ACONSU. The one exception is Alumni Connect, described below, which you have to switch on yourself.

Who can see what

Access inside ACONSU is limited by office, not given to everyone with a login:

  • Your own chapter only. Chapters are separated. A leader in one chapter cannot see another chapter's members, finances or welfare cases.
  • Welfare requests are visible to the Welfare office and the Chapter Coordinator. Case notes are never shown to the member list or to other members.
  • Pastoral notes are visible to Shepherding.
  • Financial records are visible to Finance, the Financial Secretary and the Treasurer.
  • Department information is visible to the head of that department, not to other departments.
  • Prayer requests may be shared with the prayer team, and appear publicly on the prayer wall only if you ask for that when submitting.
  • National officers see chapter totals and readiness figures, not individual member records.
  • Alumni Connect is the one directory that spans chapters, and only for alumni who switched it on themselves — see below.

Alumni Connect

Members who have finished their studies can choose to appear in Alumni Connect, a directory other members use to find ACONSU graduates by profession. This is the only part of the service that is visible across chapters, and it works differently from everything else here:

  • It is off unless you turn it on. Nothing about you appears there until you write your own entry and tick "List me". No account is added automatically.
  • You write it yourself — what you do, where you work, your field, what you studied, the year you finished, your city, and anything you want to say about your work.
  • Your contact details are shown only if you say so. Your email and phone number are not published just because we hold them; each has its own switch.
  • It is visible to full members of the union, from any chapter. Visitors and people still being welcomed cannot see it.
  • You can take it down at any time, from your profile, and it disappears immediately. Your Chapter Coordinator can also remove a listing belonging to your chapter.

If you never turn it on, nothing about you is ever shown outside your chapter.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising. We share it only with the providers who run the service for us:

  • Render — hosting for the application
  • MongoDB Atlas — the database where records are stored
  • mNotify — SMS delivery, when your chapter sends a text. Only the phone number and the message are passed.
  • An email provider — password-reset emails and notifications to the office
  • Google and Mozilla push services — delivering notifications to your device, if you turn them on

We also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and membership record: for as long as you are a member, and afterwards as alumni history unless you ask us to remove it
  • Attendance and giving records: kept as part of the chapter's own records
  • Welfare case notes: kept while the case is open and for a reasonable period after it closes
  • SMS and notification logs: kept so the office can see what was actually sent
  • Password-reset tokens: expire shortly after they are issued

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you what we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong — most of it you can edit yourself on your profile
  • Delete your account and the personal information attached to it
  • Stop sending you notifications, emails or SMS
  • Withdraw your consent, which means closing your account

Ask through the contact page and we will respond within 30 days. Some records we may need to keep even after an account is closed — financial records in particular — but they will no longer be linked to an active profile.

Security

  • Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes and are never recoverable, by us or anyone else
  • Access is restricted by office, and a leader's session ends when their term of office ends
  • Leadership terms run for one academic year, after which access stops automatically

No service can promise perfect security. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Children

This service is for university students and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will remove the account.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell members through the app if the change is significant.
