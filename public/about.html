Who We Are

About ACONSU

The Apostles' Continuation Students Union — a family of students, on campus for a purpose bigger than a degree.

Our Story

ACONSU exists to see students encounter God and continue steadfastly in the pattern the apostles left the early church: the Word, fellowship, prayer, and one another. We believe campus life is fertile ground for a generation to be raised — not just academically, but spiritually.

What We Believe

We hold to the Scriptures as the final authority for faith and living, and we exist to help every student grow in Christ, discover their gifts, and carry that fire into every hostel, lecture hall and city they go on to.
