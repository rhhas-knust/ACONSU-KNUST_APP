Cookies and storage

Last updated: 6 October 2026

We use only what the service needs in order to work. We use no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, so there is no cookie banner and nothing for you to accept or refuse. If we ever add something that is not strictly necessary, we will ask for your permission first and update this page.

Videos and other companies

A YouTube or Facebook video is not loaded until you press its button, so those companies set no cookies and learn nothing about you before then. Once you press it, their policies apply to that video. Fonts, scripts and pictures come from this app itself.

Your control

You can clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. Signing out ends the session cookie straight away.
