Giving and refunds

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This page explains how giving works in the ACONSU app and what happens if a gift is sent in error. It is not legal advice, and your chapter's finance office has the final say on each case.

How giving works

  • The app does not take payments. It shows the chapter's Mobile Money or bank details, and you send the money from your own MoMo or banking app.
  • You can then log the gift in the app. The Finance office checks your entry against the chapter's account before it is confirmed.
  • Because the money moves between you and your own provider, any fee they charge is theirs, and ACONSU never sees your PIN or card details.

Gifts are voluntary

Tithes, offerings, harvest and welfare gifts are freely given. Once a gift has been received and used for the purpose you gave it, it cannot be refunded.

When we will refund

Tell us within 14 days of sending if:

  • you sent the wrong amount (for example an extra zero);
  • the same gift was sent twice;
  • you sent it to the wrong chapter or the wrong number because of something on our pages.

The Finance office will check it against the books. If the money has not yet been used, it will be returned to you by the same route it came from (the same MoMo number or bank account). We will tell you the outcome. If we cannot return it, we will tell you why.

How to ask

Use the contact page and give the date, amount, the number or account you sent from, and your transaction reference if you have it. Never send a PIN or password.

Disputes with your provider

If money left your account without your permission, contact your mobile money provider or bank first. They hold the transaction and can reverse or investigate it. We will help by confirming what we received.

Events and other charges

The app does not currently charge for events. If a chapter ever collects a fee, it will say so on the event, and this page will say how that is refunded.
